# Breaking Bad — Episodes Breakdown (Key)

## 1x01: Pilot
Walter learns of his diagnosis and begins his descent into the underworld.

## 2x06: Peekaboo
Jesse encounters a neglected child, highlighting his moral struggle.

## 3x07: One Minute
Hank faces his past and battles the cousins.

## 4x10: Salud
Gus Fring takes control of the cartel in a brutal act of vengeance.

## 5x14: Ozymandias
The climax of the series: Hank dies, and Walter's life collapses.
