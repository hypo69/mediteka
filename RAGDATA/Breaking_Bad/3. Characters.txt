# Breaking Bad — Characters

## Walter White (Heisenberg)
Central character, transforming from a humble chemistry teacher into a ruthless drug lord. Driven by pride and a desire for recognition after his cancer diagnosis, he loses his humanity, destroying his family in the process.

## Jesse Pinkman
Walter's former student and reluctant business partner. A complex, traumatized character who retains remnants of morality and compassion, suffering from the consequences of their actions and Walter's manipulations.

## Skyler White
Walter's wife. Initially a protective mother, she is forced to become a reluctant accomplice, trapped in a web of lies to save her children.

## Hank Schrader
DEA agent and Walter's brother-in-law. A symbol of justice who is constantly one step away from uncovering Walter's secret.

## Gustavo Fring
A calculated, cold, and disciplined drug lord who masks his business behind the "Los Pollos Hermanos" restaurant chain.
