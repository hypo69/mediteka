# Breaking Bad — General Information

## Main Details
**Title:** Breaking Bad
**Year of Release:** 2008–2013
**Number of Seasons:** 5
**Number of Episodes:** 62
**Country of Production:** USA
**Genre:** Crime drama, neo-western, thriller, dark comedy
**Main Cast:** Bryan Cranston (Walter White), Aaron Paul (Jesse Pinkman), Anna Gunn (Skyler White), Dean Norris (Hank Schrader), Betsy Brandt (Marie Schrader), RJ Mitte (Walter White Jr.), Bob Odenkirk (Saul Goodman), Giancarlo Esposito (Gustavo Fring)

## General Plot Overview
The story begins with Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher living in Albuquerque, who is diagnosed with inoperable lung cancer. Faced with the prospect of leaving his pregnant wife Skyler and son Walter Jr., who has cerebral palsy, without financial support, he makes a desperate decision. Using his deep knowledge of chemistry, he begins producing high-quality methamphetamine. He partners with a former student, Jesse Pinkman, a small-time drug dealer. Gradually, their amateur business turns into a dangerous drug empire. Walter adopts the criminal pseudonym "Heisenberg." As the plot progresses, Walter descends deeper into the criminal world, committing increasingly brutal acts. The story evolves into a drama about the transformation of a man who, under the influence of terminal illness and his own pride, gradually loses his humanity.
