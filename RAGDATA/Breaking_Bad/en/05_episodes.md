# Episodes Breakdown: Breaking Bad (Key)

*Note: Smart scanning applies for long series.*

## 1x01: Pilot
Walter learns of his diagnosis and begins his descent into the underworld.
**Final:** Walter and Jesse commit their first serious crime.

## 2x06: Peekaboo
Jesse encounters a neglected child, highlighting his moral struggle.
**Final:** Jesse calls the police to save the child.

## 3x07: One Minute
Hank faces his past and battles the cousins.
**Final:** Hank kills the cousins but suffers severe injuries.

## 4x10: Salud
Gus Fring takes control of the cartel in a brutal act of vengeance.
**Final:** The cartel leadership is liquidated.

## 5x14: Ozymandias
The climax of the series: Hank dies, and Walter's life collapses.
**Final:** Walter flees, leaving his old life behind forever.
