# Breaking Bad — Locations

## Albuquerque, New Mexico
The main setting. Its desert landscapes and harsh light create the unique atmosphere of isolation.

## Walter White's House
An iconic real house in Albuquerque, symbol of Walter's double life.

## RV (Mobile Lab)
The location where Walter and Jesse started, symbolizing their path of crime and isolation.

## Los Pollos Hermanos
A front for Gus Fring's drug business; symbolizes the mask of legitimacy.
