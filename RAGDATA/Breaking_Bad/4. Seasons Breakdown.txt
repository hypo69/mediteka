# Breaking Bad — Seasons Breakdown

## Season 1 (2008)
Walter White is diagnosed with cancer and teams up with Jesse to cook meth.
**Final:** Walter takes the name Heisenberg and establishes his presence in the drug underworld.

## Season 2 (2009)
The business grows, but so do the risks. Family life deteriorates.
**Final:** A plane crash over Albuquerque results from the chain of events Walter started.

## Season 3 (2010)
Walter works for Gus Fring. Conflict rises between them.
**Final:** Walter kills Gale Boetticher to survive.

## Season 4 (2011)
A chess game between Walter and Gus.
**Final:** Walter kills Gus Fring and declares, "I won."

## Season 5 (2012–2013)
The empire crumbles, and the truth is revealed.
**Final:** Walter dies in his lab, leaving a trail of destruction behind him.
